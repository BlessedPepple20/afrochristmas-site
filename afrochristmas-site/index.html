FAQ

Good questions

Where and when is AfroChristmas 2.0?

Saturday 12 December 2026 at Loveworld Center, Odums, along East-West Road, Port Harcourt. Red carpet 2PM, event starts 3PM.

Is the event ticketed?

Yes. Choose a pass from Regular (₦5,000) up to Royal Table (₦500,000+).

What does my pass include?

Every pass card above lists exactly what you get, from general access to reserved tables.

How do I receive my QR code?

Register, pay by transfer, send proof on WhatsApp. Once confirmed, your QR pass is sent to your email and WhatsApp.

What if I lose my QR code?

Message us on WhatsApp with your Registration ID (AC2-XXXXXX) and we will resend it.

Can I transfer my ticket or get a refund?

Contact the organisers on WhatsApp before the event. Tell us the new name for transfers.

Can I register as a vendor or designer? Can I bring my own model?

Yes to both. Designers can bring their own models or request models, subject to availability.

What time should I arrive?

2PM for the red carpet, 3PM when the show starts.
